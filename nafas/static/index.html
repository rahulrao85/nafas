How the loop works

  1. Notice. Each morning the engine scores last night and looks for a lapse (2 nights off, or 3 of 5 under goal), a slow decline (14-night average under 80% of the 28 before), a large leak, or leak creeping up.
  2. Name the barrier. It guesses why from the data — leak, mid-night removal, a trip (watch saw sleep, CPAP didn't), a moved bedtime — and shows the evidence. Your own answer always wins.
  3. One small step. One action from a reviewed library, tagged with its behaviour-change technique, with why it should help and where it stops helping.
  4. Judge and learn. Three nights later it checks whether things improved and updates what works for you. Two misses in a row on the same barrier move to a care-team step.
  5. Keep progress. Banked nights never go down. A lone short night doesn't break your run. A lapse opens a comeback goal of one hour, not a reset to zero.

Safety lines

  • Never suggests a pressure change, medication, or stopping therapy. High residual AHI goes to your care team, in fixed wording.
  • AI only words the note. Drafts that break a rule are replaced by the reviewed template.
  • Notes that mention chest pain, trouble breathing or self-harm show an emergency message instead.

Your data

  • The demo runs on synthetic, fictional people only.
  • Uploads are parsed in memory for this session and deleted when you press Delete or the server stops.
  • Nothing is shared unless you turn on care-team sharing, and even then only a summary you can see first.
